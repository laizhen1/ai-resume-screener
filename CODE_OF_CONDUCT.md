# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful project where people can participate regardless of identity, background or experience.

Be constructive, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy, and avoid harassment or discriminatory language. Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not include personal or candidate data in a report.
